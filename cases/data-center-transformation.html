Case Study

End-to-end data center transformation for Central Bank of Egypt

Credit Guarantee Corporation (CBE ecosystem)

Financial services Infrastructure Digital transformation
Full Greenfield data center delivery

Tier Enterprise-grade availability design

Challenge

Standing up regulated infrastructure from the ground up

Credit Guarantee Corporation required a complete data center and enterprise information infrastructure to support its role within Egypt’s banking ecosystem — with expectations set by Central Bank of Egypt oversight. Legacy facilities and ad-hoc systems could not meet availability, security, or audit requirements for financial-sector operations.

The organization needed a trusted partner to translate regulatory and business requirements into a deployable architecture — spanning physical infrastructure, networking, compute, storage, backup, and operational runbooks.

Solution

Infrastructure and transformation consulting, delivered end to end

Digital Hub provided infrastructure and digital transformation consulting — leading discovery, design, procurement support, implementation oversight, and handover for a full data center build-out and supporting enterprise information systems.

  • Data center facility planning — power, cooling, rack layout, and physical security
  • Network, compute, and storage architecture with redundancy and DR alignment
  • Enterprise information infrastructure — directory services, monitoring, backup, and operations tooling
  • Migration planning and cutover support for critical workloads

Our consultants worked alongside internal IT and vendor teams to ensure every layer — from raised floor to application connectivity — met CBE-aligned governance and operational readiness criteria.

Results

Production-ready infrastructure for financial-sector workloads

The Credit Guarantee Corporation now operates on a purpose-built data center platform designed for resilience, auditability, and growth. Teams gained standardized environments, documented procedures, and visibility into capacity and health — reducing reliance on fragmented, high-risk legacy setups.

The transformation established a foundation for future digital initiatives — core systems integration, analytics, and partner connectivity — without revisiting fundamental infrastructure decisions. Digital Hub’s engagement model combined strategic advisory with hands-on delivery accountability expected in regulated banking contexts.
