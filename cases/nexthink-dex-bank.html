Challenge

Blind spots across a distributed banking workforce

A leading Egyptian bank operated hundreds of endpoints across branches, headquarters, and remote roles — yet IT lacked unified visibility into device health, application performance, and the digital experience employees actually encountered day to day. Reactive support tickets and siloed monitoring tools made it difficult to prioritize fixes or prove service quality to business stakeholders.

Leadership wanted a single platform to understand endpoint friction before it became outages — aligned with modern Digital Employee Experience (DEX) practices and Nexthink’s industry-leading observability model.

Solution

Enterprise-wide Nexthink DEX deployment

As a Nexthink Authorized Reseller, Digital Hub designed and executed a full-environment rollout of Nexthink DEX — from architecture and agent deployment through dashboard configuration, integrations, and IT operations enablement.

  • Discovery and scoping across branches, HQ, and hybrid work patterns
  • Phased agent rollout to 500+ endpoints with minimal business disruption
  • DEX scorecards, alerts, and executive views tailored to banking IT workflows
  • Knowledge transfer so internal teams can run, tune, and expand the platform

Deployment aligned with the bank’s security and change-management processes — ensuring DEX telemetry complemented existing SIEM and service desk operations rather than replacing them.

Results

Proactive IT with measurable employee experience

IT teams now operate with live visibility into endpoint health and employee experience at scale — shifting from ticket-driven firefighting to data-informed remediation. Recurring friction points surfaced through DEX analytics, enabling targeted fixes that improved satisfaction and reduced mean time to resolution.

The bank established a sustainable DEX practice supported by Digital Hub’s Nexthink expertise — with room to expand monitoring, automate remediation playbooks, and tie experience metrics to broader service management goals across the organization.
