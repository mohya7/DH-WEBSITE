Case Study

3+ years powering Egypt’s national planning systems

Ministry of Planning & Economic Development

Government Data & Analytics Enterprise Software
3+ Years of continuous engagement
1 National data warehouse platform
24/7 Platform support & operations
Multi Agency dashboards & reporting layers
Challenge

Modernizing national planning on legacy foundations

The Ministry needed a single, authoritative view of economic and development planning data across departments — while meeting strict cybersecurity requirements and keeping mission-critical systems available around the clock. Fragmented sources, manual reporting cycles, and aging infrastructure made it difficult for leadership to act on timely, trusted intelligence.

Digital Hub was engaged to design and deliver the full stack: enterprise data warehouse, hardened security posture, executive dashboards, and sustained platform operations aligned with public-sector governance.

Solution

End-to-end platform ownership from warehouse to operations

Our team led the development of Egyptian planning systems end to end — consolidating disparate feeds into a governed data warehouse, implementing cybersecurity controls and monitoring, and building advanced dashboards tailored to ministry leadership and technical teams.

  • Enterprise data warehouse architecture with ETL pipelines and data quality rules
  • Cybersecurity hardening, access control, and operational security practices
  • Executive and operational dashboards for planning, monitoring, and reporting
  • Dedicated platform support — incident response, releases, and capacity planning

Engagement models combined on-site delivery with offshore engineering capacity, giving the Ministry predictable velocity without sacrificing accountability to national stakeholders.

Results

Trusted planning intelligence at national scale

After three years of partnership, the Ministry operates on a unified data foundation that supports strategic planning cycles, cross-agency coordination, and evidence-based policy decisions. Leadership dashboards replaced weeks of manual consolidation with near-real-time visibility into key indicators.

Security and support SLAs keep critical systems resilient — enabling the organization to focus on economic development outcomes rather than infrastructure firefighting. The engagement remains active, with Digital Hub continuing to evolve the platform as planning priorities shift.
