From pilots to production: what Egyptian enterprises get wrong about AI deployment

AI pilots are easy to launch. Production AI requires governance, integration, data quality, security, and ownership.

The pilot trap

Many organizations prove that an AI model can answer a question, summarize content, or automate a task. The harder question is whether that workflow can be trusted inside real operations.

Production AI must handle permissions, auditability, data freshness, exception handling, and human approval paths.

The gap between a demo and a production AI workflow is usually not the model. It is the operating model around it.

What production needs

  • Clear business owner and measurable target outcome.
  • Trusted data sources and rules for sensitive information.
  • Integration with existing systems, not a standalone experiment.
  • Monitoring, feedback loops, and human-in-the-loop controls.

Enterprises that succeed treat AI as a delivery program: data, software, security, and change management moving together.
