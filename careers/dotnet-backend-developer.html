.NET Backend developer

Hybrid - Internship Cairo, New Cairo

Posted 2 days ago

Job Description

We are seeking a talented and motivated fresh grad to join our development team as a .NET Backend Intern. This role offers an excellent opportunity to gain hands-on experience in software development and contribute to real-world projects.

Responsibilities

  • Assist in the development and maintenance of backend applications using .NET technologies.
  • Collaborate with the development team to understand project requirements and design solutions.
  • Write clean, efficient, and maintainable code.
  • Learn and apply best practices in software development.

Requirements

  • Graduated from Computer Science, Information Technology, or a related field.
  • Strong understanding of programming concepts and data structures.
  • Proficiency in C# and .NET framework.
  • Knowledge of SQL and database design is a plus.
  • Ability to learn new technologies quickly.
  • Strong problem-solving and analytical skills.
  • Excellent communication and teamwork abilities.

All the above requirements and responsibilities are crucial for you to be able to take on in this role. We are looking for someone who has the energy and passion to bring together new ideas.
