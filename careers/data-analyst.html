Data Analyst

On-Site - project-based internship Cairo, New Cairo

Posted 2 days ago

Job Description

We are seeking a highly motivated and analytical fresh graduate to join our data team as a Data Analyst Intern. This role provides an excellent opportunity to gain hands-on experience in data analysis, visualization, and problem-solving.

Responsibilities

  • Assist in data collection, cleaning, and preparation for analysis.
  • Conduct exploratory data analysis to identify patterns and trends.
  • Develop and maintain data visualizations using tools like Tableau, Power BI, or Python libraries.
  • Support data-driven decision making by providing insights and recommendations.
  • Assist in building and maintaining data pipelines.
  • Collaborate with cross-functional teams to understand data requirements.

Requirements

  • Bachelor degree in Statistics, Mathematics, Economics, Computer Science, or a related field.
  • Strong analytical and problem-solving skills.
  • Proficiency in data analysis tools (Excel, SQL, Python, R).
  • Basic understanding of statistical concepts.
  • Ability to communicate complex data insights clearly and effectively.
  • Strong attention to detail and accuracy.

All the above requirements and responsibilities are crucial for you to be able to take on in this role. We are looking for someone who has the energy and passion to bring together new ideas.
